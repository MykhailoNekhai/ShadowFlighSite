About The Game

ShadowFlight is a dark atmospheric platformer built around movement, timing, and survival. You fly through hand-crafted levels filled with deadly obstacles, strange power-ups, and unpredictable physics-based challenges.

The game is simple to understand, but every level pushes you harder. Sometimes you need patience. Sometimes you need speed. Sometimes one mistake is enough to lose everything. The deeper you go, the darker and more dangerous the world becomes.

Use power-ups to clone yourself, change size, speed up, slow down, and survive situations that look impossible at first. Play alone or join a friend in co-op mode and face the darkness together.

Gameplay

Fly, dodge, react, and adapt.

ShadowFlight is not only about reaching the end of the level — it is about surviving the journey. Each stage introduces new hazards, new rhythms, and new ways to test your control.

The game rewards precision, timing, and quick thinking. Every trap has a pattern. Every obstacle has a way through. You just have to find it before the darkness catches you.
